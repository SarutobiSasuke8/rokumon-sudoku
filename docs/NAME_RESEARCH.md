# Name research — 2026-09-04

## Recommendation

Do **not** use **Ultimate Sudoku** as the public launch name. It is descriptive, difficult to own in search, and already used by multiple Sudoku games.

Current collisions found:

- CodeThisLab's browser game **Ultimate Sudoku**, distributed by Poki and sold on CodeCanyon: <https://poki.com/en/g/ultimate-sudoku> and <https://codecanyon.net/item/ultimate-sudoku-html5-game/11292951>
- The 2005 Windows title **Ultimate Sudoku**: <https://www.mobygames.com/game/246434/ultimate-sudoku/>
- **Ultimate Sudoku Collection** on Steam: <https://store.steampowered.com/app/851810/Ultimate_Sudoku_Collection/>
- Current App Store listings using **Ultimate Sudoku**: <https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ultimate-sudoku-app/id6743371040>

This is a practical availability assessment, not legal advice or formal trademark clearance.

## Preferred direction

**Rokumon Sudoku**

- Leads with the existing Rokumon Games brand.
- Keeps **The Six Gates** available as the campaign identity without overloading the product title.
- Retains “Sudoku” for category discovery.
- Search checks found no obvious exact-title game collision at review time.

Selected by the product owner on 2026-09-04.

## Other viable candidates

1. **Rokumon Sudoku: Path of Nine** — compact and progression-led.
2. **The Sixth Grid** — ownable, but weaker category clarity.
3. **Nine Gates** — strong campaign identity, but needs a Sudoku subtitle.
4. **Ink & Nine** — polished and theme-friendly, less obviously adventurous.
5. **Rokumon Gridmaster** — game-like, though “Gridmaster” is crowded.

Avoid **Sudoku Odyssey** and **Ninefold**; both are used by active Sudoku products.

## Clearance before launch

Run exact and confusingly-similar searches in the UK IPO, EUIPO, USPTO and WIPO databases for the intended territories and classes, then check domains, social handles and app stores. Have counsel review the final candidate if this becomes a commercial release.
