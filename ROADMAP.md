# Roadmap

- **Vertical slice — current:** campaign and quick-create modes, four variants, scoring, progression, themes and hub events.
- **Content alpha:** 36 curated campaign puzzles, technique tutorials, resume, records, accessibility settings and analytics.
- **Variant expansion:** Killer, Jigsaw and Even/Odd; daily calendar; unlockable cosmetics; optional PWA.
- **Release candidate:** device matrix, campaign solve-log QA, performance and accessibility gates, title clearance.
- **Live:** evidence-led tuning from completion, assistance, abandonment and retention data.

See `docs/PRODUCT_PLAN.md` for scope, risks and launch criteria.
